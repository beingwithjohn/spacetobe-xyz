What it is for

Make friends with experience.

Trusting yourself is not confidence that things will always go perfectly, and it is not certainty about who you are. It is closer to a friendship, with yourself and life.

Meditation, or sitting, is a way of turning towards our experience and befriending it. Over time, a sitting practice can shift the atmosphere of experience towards the qualities of friendship: gentleness, precision and humour.

Beyond Belief is a guided exploration into befriending your direct experience. You are invited to come along and see what you discover.

Why this long

Enough time for it to become yours.

Over thirty-five days there is time to grow in confidence with sitting and find your own ways of making the practice yours.

Ten people maximum. Small enough to go deep and get into what meditation really is.

The shape of it

Five ways of approaching, in an order that matters.

These are not five topics. They are how a friendship gets built: one quality at a time, each week making the next one possible.

01

Curiosity

Opening toward what is actually here, and discovering it.

02

Care

Gentleness and warmth, turned toward yourself first, then outwards.

03

Responsibility

The ability to respond to what arises and meet need.

04

Play

Giving yourself the generosity of “no wrong way to do it”.

05

Rest

Pausing every striving, and recognising space uncontrived and open.

06

The closing

We look back over the thirty-five days together, and close.
